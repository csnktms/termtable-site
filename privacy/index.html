Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 August 2026

Termtable is built on a simple principle: your timetable is your business. This policy explains, completely, what happens with your data — which is quick, because almost nothing does.

What we collect

Nothing. Termtable has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and makes no network requests. We do not collect, receive, store, or share any personal data — or any data at all — from your use of the app.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter into Termtable — academic years, terms, classes, locations, notes, settings — is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is never transmitted anywhere. Widgets and the Live Activity read the same on-device data; class reminders are local notifications scheduled entirely by your device. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it.

Third parties

There are none. Termtable includes no third-party code, SDKs, analytics services, or advertising frameworks.

Apple

Termtable is distributed through the App Store and TestFlight, which are operated by Apple. Apple may process data related to your download or beta participation (such as crash reports or TestFlight feedback you choose to send) under Apple's privacy policy. If you share TestFlight feedback or a crash report, we may see it — that's the only way any information from you can reach us through the app, and it happens only at your explicit choice.

If you email us

If you contact support at support@termtable.app, we'll have your email address and whatever you write — used only to reply and resolve your issue, kept only as long as ordinary correspondence, never shared.

Children

Termtable is safe for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

If a future version of Termtable ever changes any of the above (for example, an optional iCloud sync feature), this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be clearly described.
